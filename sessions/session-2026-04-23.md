## Topics covered
Transactions!
They are a way of performing multiple operations as a single unit, so everything is interconnected on whether they work together correctly or fail completely.

## What I understood
A transaction is a group of database operations that must be executed as a single unit (all is saved or nothing is saved).

Commit: Saves all changes permanently.

Rollback : Undoes changes if something goes wrong until the last commit.

Update : Inside the transaction modify the data.
